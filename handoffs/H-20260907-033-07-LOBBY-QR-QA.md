## New blocker found

Required renderer-failure QA exposed a second client defect in `client/src/qr-runtime.ts`.

When `window.QRCode` is unavailable, `decorateLobby()` renders the fallback and sets `data-qr-ready="0"`, but its `host.innerHTML` mutation triggers the global `MutationObserver`, which schedules `refresh()` again. The only same-URL short-circuit is for `qrReady === "1"`, so fallback is rewritten repeatedly. Browser fallback runs consistently hang in the QRCode-unavailable path, and the source control flow deterministically explains the loop.
