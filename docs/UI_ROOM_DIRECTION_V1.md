## Final refinements that superseded the intermediate direction

The user subsequently approved the following concrete details:

- Minimap upper-right.
- Default compact minimap target approximately 200×140 desktop and always renders real map information.
- Click minimap locations to pan/recenter camera.
- Government and local Home/Residence are highlighted/clickable on the minimap for fast camera focus.
- Old separate Government/Home quick-nav buttons are removed from the World Map.
- Bottom-right keeps only Zoom In/Zoom Out controls with reduced idle opacity.
- Government uses a radial/circular central plaza.
- Residence density is balanced/natural.
- Turn Track uses individual left-edge avatar tokens with one vertical line passing through their centers; no substantial panel background.
- Desktop Residence/Government/world information surfaces use floating middle-right cards/panels rather than fixed full-height drawers.
- Map dims lightly while the floating panel is open and remains persistent.
- Mobile keeps a smaller always-informational minimap.

All other final requirements and acceptance criteria live in `docs/UI_ROOM_APPROVED_V1.md`.
